Support

Need help with FitFunction? Email fitfunction@proton.me and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Report a problem

Tell us what happened and what you expected. It helps to include your iPhone model and iOS version, and a screenshot if you can.

Restore FitFunction Pro

If you bought Pro and it isn't unlocked, open Settings, tap Open Paywall, then tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple ID that made the purchase. Purchases, refunds and billing are handled by Apple.

Back up your workouts

Your routines and history are stored only on your iPhone. Open Settings and tap Back Up Data to save a backup file to iCloud Drive or Files. You can bring it back later with Restore from Backup, even on a new phone. If the app is deleted without a backup, we can't recover your data.
