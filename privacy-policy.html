Privacy Policy

FitFunction · Last updated October 19, 2026

FitFunction doesn't collect, sell, or share your personal information. Your workouts stay on your device.

What stays on your phone

Everything you create or log in FitFunction is stored only on your device:

• Saved routines and edited workouts

• Your workout history, including weights, reps, times, and scores

• Your settings, such as timer sound and countdown length

There are no accounts or sign-ins, and we have no server that holds your data. We can't see your workouts.

What we collect

Nothing. FitFunction has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party software that collects data. The app does not send your information over the internet.

Purchases

If you buy FitFunction Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple ID.

Notifications

When you run a timer, FitFunction can ask permission to send notifications so you hear about the end of a workout while your phone is locked. These are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Backups and sharing

Backups and sharing only happen when you choose them. A backup is a file saved wherever you pick, such as iCloud Drive or Files. When you share a workout as text, an image, or a PDF, it goes to the app or person you select, and their handling of it is covered by their own policies.

Your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups may also include FitFunction's data. Those are managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

FitFunction is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at fitfunction@proton.me.
